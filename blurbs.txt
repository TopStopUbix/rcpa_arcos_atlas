# EU
A contient of Faustian ambitions, Europe is the nucleus of Pax Auctoritas which birthed the secretive Auctoritas Imperata and its rebirth into the Righteous Central Protection Auctority. In persuit of God and Glory, the nations of Europe drove magnificient advancement yet also seeded its own ruination. After withnessing the immolation of European civilization over the course of the Great War, the renewed CENTRAL had endeavoured to prevent the outbreak of a second, more devestating war. 

Teetering the edge of destruction once more, CENTRAL took its first step out from the shadows for the first time in 1939, when it began to administer the reawakened Germany. CENTRAL intervened for a second time in 1943 in defense of the Polish nation from Soviet aggression. 

Intervention after intervention, nation after nation, the states of Europe ultimately came under the CENTRAL's rule as today's most integrated parts of the Pax Auctoritas. 

Overseen by a dozen Regional Directors and sited with hundreds more Command Pyramids so emblemetic of CENTRAL's omnipresence, guiding policies are drawn up and first implemented here, to then be exported into farther administrations with laxer degrees of control.

# LA
As a land taken without conflict, Latin America is source to much political unrest and dissident thought - areas which have never truly been conquered, betrayed by their old leaders. The Central's mandate system through which it controls Latin American and African countries allows for a modicum of independence that, while not sizable thanks to economic and resource pressure, has spawned a bloated political balance to profit off discontent. Workers’ syndicates and state representatives lease political stability in exchange for payment and resources, and exhort riots if demands are not met.

This has an important consequence. As political balance is largely kept by this system, less armed forces are required to suppress rioting: it is common for Latin American secforce battalions to be “loaned” to other portions of the RCPA experiencing major political unrest, among them the Vespucci Devastated Territories Reconstruction Trust and the Free Chinese front. In turn, this means ideological repression is somewhat lax compared to places in which Central is better established, creating numerous minor havens for dissidence: dens in which ideological discontent continues to breed.

# AF
The crown jewel of Central’s old guard: modern Africa is mostly constituted of various states and mandates, the strongest of which are mostly autonomous and democratic in governance. Here lies the breadbasket of Central, left untouched by former mismanagement with old colonies peacefully ceded by European opportunists hoping for colonialism to thrive under the RCPA’s watchful eyes. In the present day, conflict is mostly resolved via treaties and checks between colonialist and anticolonialist forces alike, with anomalous support systems and larger contraceptive availability helping transition Africa out of its tumultuous past. It is the shining example of the compromises of a brighter age, in which Central did more than throw bodies and anomalies at every problem, and in which wars could be won without ceaseless sacrifice and the grinding of ideals to disillusionment.
